Privacy policy

Rust & Revival. Last updated November 1, 2026.

Rust & Revival has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name, email address or location. Your game is saved on your device.

Ads

The game offers optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information, your IP address, your device's advertising identifier and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first, and on iPhone it uses Apple's tracking prompt. You can change your choice from Privacy options in the game's store, when that option applies where you live. How Google uses data in ads.

Purchases

Wrench packs are sold through Google Play or the App Store, which handle your payment. We never see your card details. RevenueCat processes purchase records, tied to an anonymous ID, so the game can deliver what you bought. RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Your data

Deleting the game removes the save on your device. To ask about data held by Google or RevenueCat, use the links above or contact us.
